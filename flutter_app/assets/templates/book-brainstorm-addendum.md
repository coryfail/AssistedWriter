## Brainstorming notes

The in-app assistant can propose up to eight notes for `notes/book.md`, the current chapter's `notesFile`, or the character, location, timeline, and terminology trackers. It chooses a destination for each proposed note. The author approves each note separately before the app appends it as a `##` heading with Markdown content. Proposed ideas are possibilities, not established story facts. When helping outside the app, keep suggestions distinct from confirmed notes until the author chooses what to save.
